# RJ Services & RJ CMS 5.1 Migration

This document explains the migration path from the legacy RJ CMS 5.1 and the services offered under RJ Services.

## 1. RJ CMS 5.1 Deprecation & Migration
**Important:** RJ CMS version 5.1 is officially discontinued and no longer maintained. If you are currently using RJ CMS 5.1, you must upgrade to either **RJ CMS AI** or **RJ CMS Nexus** to ensure security and continued support.

### Upgrading to RJ CMS Nexus (Free & Open Source)
When you decide to upgrade from RJ CMS 5.1 to RJ CMS Nexus, the transition is completely **free**. 
*   **Data Safety:** None of your existing data, posts, pages, or settings will be lost during the migration.
*   **Themes & Plugins:** Any premium themes and plugins you purchased for RJ CMS 5.1 will be migrated automatically. 
*   **Updates:** As long as your theme or plugin license is still active, you will continue to receive updates in RJ Nexus.
*   **Legacy Marketplace:** Because RJ Nexus is fully open-source, the old closed marketplace of RJ CMS 5.1 is no longer available. You cannot buy *new* legacy themes or plugins from the 5.1 ecosystem.

## 2. RJ Services Overview
**RJ Services** is a dedicated sidebar section in the admin panel where you can request professional assistance or expand your software ecosystem.

### What can you do in RJ Services?
1.  **Custom Development:** Order exclusive custom themes, plugins, or specific features tailored to your business needs.
2.  **RJ Nexus Custom Version:** Purchase a customized, white-labeled, or enterprise version of RJ Nexus.
3.  **Product Upgrades:** Upgrade your RJ Nexus installation to other advanced RJ CMS products, including:
    *   **RJ Cloud** (Cloud-hosted version)
    *   **RJ Shop** (E-commerce platform)
    *   **RJ CMS AI** (Advanced AI-integrated version)

### How to access RJ Services
In the upcoming release, a new **RJ Services** menu will be available in the admin sidebar. From there, you can contact the development team, submit custom requests, and manage your product licenses.
