# Comments Management

This document explains how to manage comments in RJ CMS.

## Viewing Comments
Go to **Comments** in the sidebar to view all comments. You can filter them by status: All, Pending, Approved, Spam, and Trash.

## How to Approve or Trash a comment
1. Hover over a comment in the list.
2. Click **Approve** to make it visible on the site, or **Trash** to move it to the trash bin.

## How to Reply to a comment
1. Click **Edit** on an approved comment.
2. Type your reply in the text area and click **Submit Reply**.

## AI Spam Filter
If AI features are enabled, RJ CMS can automatically analyze comment text to detect spam. You can also click **AI Analyze** on a specific comment to get a spam score and reasoning.

## Bulk Actions
Select multiple comments using the checkboxes, choose an action (Approve, Unapprove, Spam, Trash), and click Apply.
