# Posts Management

This document explains how to manage blog posts in RJ CMS.

## How to create a new post
1. Log in to the admin panel.
2. Go to **Posts** in the left sidebar.
3. Click the **Add New** button.
4. Enter a title and content for your post.
5. You can choose to write in the Classic Editor or use **RJBuilder** (Visual Page Builder).
6. Add relevant Categories and Tags from the right sidebar.
7. Use the **AI Assistant** buttons to automatically generate SEO titles, descriptions, or focus keywords.
8. Click **Publish** to make the post live, or **Save as Draft** to save it for later.

## How to edit an existing post
1. Go to **Posts**.
2. Hover over the post you want to edit.
3. Click the **Edit** button.
4. Make your changes and click **Update**.

## How to delete a post
1. Go to **Posts**.
2. Hover over the post.
3. Click the **Trash** button to move it to the trash. 
4. If you want to permanently delete it, click the **Trash** filter at the top. You can then click **Delete Permanently** or **Restore** the post.

## Features
*   **RJBuilder:** A drag-and-drop visual editor for creating complex layouts.
*   **AI SEO:** Auto-generate meta titles, descriptions, and keyword suggestions based on your content.
*   **Multilingual:** Add translations for your posts using the language tabs at the top of the editor.
*   **Scheduling:** Schedule posts to publish at a future date and time.
*   **Categories & Tags:** Organize your content for better navigation on the frontend.
*   **Revisions:** RJ CMS automatically saves revisions so you can restore previous versions of your content.
